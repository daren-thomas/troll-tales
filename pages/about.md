Until I actually have any content worth mentioning posted here, let me quickly explain what I _intend_ this site to become:

A place for me to collect ideas, links and tools related to TTRPGs like Dungeons & Dragons 5e.

Ideally, most of what I post here will be system agnostic - I'm more interested in story stuff than actual game mechanics and intend to collect ideas and lore here.

A lot of the content being produced for people interested in this stuff is in video format. Don't get me wrong: The medium is great, it's just... not how _my_ brain works. I want text. So I'll add text. So I'll weave all these youtube videos and youtube shorts together into a tapestry here for you to dive into. And extract the core ideas I want to use for my own games.

I'd like this site, over time, to get at the core of how to tell dnd stories. How to build campaigns? Sessions? Villains? Puzzles?

**Update**: I'm revising my stance on AI as a co-creator of the content on this site. Earlier posts did have a bunch of AI-created content. I'm not dead against that, it's just it doesn't feel like _my_ content and so I don't really see the point in doing it like that anymore. 

Some of the images are still the ai-generated version and they might just stay that way. I'm still on a journey to learn how to draw and in fact, ai is helping me by providing templates for me to copy and paint. I'll try and swap out the ai-generated pictures for those one-by-one as time permits.

Some of the random tables were generated with the help of ai. I'm getting more satisfaction out of doing it myself, but I will still at times use ai to help plan or scope out a topic. I will also use it as a glorified thesaurus.
